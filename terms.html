NearChat is strictly for users aged 18 and older. Age is verified at app launch via a mandatory date-of-birth screen. The "18+" chat mode is for adults only. The "Under 17" search parameter refers to the preferred age of a chat partner, not the user's own age. By using the app you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. 3. Code of Conduct and Prohibited Content

You agree not to use NearChat for any of the following purposes:

  • Creating, distributing or possessing child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any mode — such cases are immediately reported to law enforcement and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable, and the account is permanently blocked
  • Distributing any content involving minors in a sexual or suggestive context
  • Sending spam, unsolicited advertising, phishing links or malware
  • Impersonating another person or organisation, or deceiving your chat partner
  • Sharing personal data of third parties without their consent (doxxing)
  • Distributing malicious software or viruses
  • Violating applicable local, national or international laws and regulations
  • Using the app for human trafficking, exploitation or any form of coercion
  • Attempting to circumvent moderation, bans or technical restrictions of the app

4. Child Safety (CSAM Policy)

NearChat has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content detected or reported through the app is immediately forwarded to the relevant law enforcement authorities and, where applicable, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). The offending account is permanently blocked. Reports marked "Child safety" are flagged as critical priority and reviewed first. If you encounter such content, use the in-app Report button immediately.

5. Reporting Violations

You may report a chat partner at any time using the flag (🚩) button in the top-right corner of the chat screen. Available report reasons: Spam, Insults, Inappropriate content, Child safety, Other violation. Reports include up to the last 20 messages of the session for moderator review. "Child safety" reports are flagged as critical and reviewed with highest priority. The report is sent to Firestore and processed automatically and by the moderation team.

Users who believe a block was applied in error may appeal by contacting support. Each ban is assigned a unique number displayed on the ban screen — include this number when contacting support. The Administration will review appeals within 30 days.

9. Account Deletion and Re-registration Cooldown

You may delete your account at any time via Settings → Danger zone → Delete my account and data, or by submitting a request to support.nearchat@gmail.com. Upon deletion the following are removed: your Firestore profile, Firebase Auth account, RTDB presence, queue and session records, and all local app preferences. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

A 7-day cooldown period applies after account deletion before a new account may be created from the same device. This is a security measure to prevent abuse and ban circumvention. Device identifier records tied to active bans are retained to prevent ban circumvention.

10. Data Security Measures

NearChat implements the following technical security measures to protect user data and the platform:

  • Firebase Security Rules restrict each user to reading and writing only their own data
  • Session creation is performed exclusively server-side via Cloud Functions — clients cannot create sessions directly
  • System fields (ban status, roles, admin flags) cannot be modified by client-side code
  • The banned devices list is accessible only to the Administration — not to regular users
  • Ban verification is performed after authentication using the user's own UID record
  • All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Inactive accounts are automatically deleted after 90 days of inactivity
  • Chat messages are automatically deleted within 24 hours after a session ends
